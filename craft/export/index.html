Put your build into your Minecraft world

Export from Craft Block, open it in the game, type one command. No mods, no third-party tools. Works with Bedrock (phone, tablet, console, Windows) and Java (PC and Mac).

Bedrock: phone, console, WindowsJava: PC and MacChoose the exact spot

1. Export from the app

On the home screen, press and hold a build. Choose Export to Minecraft, then the edition you play. The app shows the footprint of the build, what it will replace, the command to type — and hands you a file.

B

Bedrock gives you a .mcpack

A behavior pack. Minecraft imports it the moment you open the file.

J

Java gives you a .zip datapack

It goes into your world's folder. Nothing to install.

Premium. Export is part of Craft Block Premium. Everything else on this page is free: the game already knows how to do it.

2a. Bedrock — phone, tablet, console, Windows

Bedrock is the edition on iPhone, iPad, Android, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and the Microsoft Store on Windows.

1

Open the .mcpack

iPhone / iPad: in the Files app tap the file, or tap Share and pick Minecraft. Android: tap the file in your file manager. Windows: double-click it. Minecraft starts and says Import successful.

2

Turn on cheats in the world

Open the world's settings (the pencil next to it). Under Game switch on Activate Cheats. This turns off achievements for that world — use a copy if you care.

3

Add the pack to the world

Still in the world's settings: Behavior Packs → Available → find the pack named after your build → Activate.

4

Stand where the build should go and type the command

Open chat (the speech bubble, or T on a keyboard) and type the command the app showed you. It looks like this — the last part is your build's name:

/function craft_my_house

2b. Java — PC and Mac

Java is the edition from the Minecraft Launcher on Windows, Mac and Linux.

1

Get the .zip onto the computer

AirDrop, email, a cable — any way. Do not unzip it.

2

Put it in your world's datapacks folder

Every world has one. On Mac: in Finder press ⌘⇧G and paste ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves. On Windows: press Win+R and open %appdata%\.minecraft\saves. Open the folder with your world's name, then datapacks (create it if it is not there), and drop the zip in.

Easier, if the world is new: on the Create New World screen go to More → Data Packs → Open Pack Folder, drop the zip there, move it to Selected. Then skip step 3.

3

Switch the pack on

An existing world does not enable a new pack by itself. In the game, open chat with T and type these two lines, one after the other. The file name is the one you dropped in:

/datapack enable "file/my_house_datapack.zip"/reload
4

Stand where the build should go and type the command

The app showed it to you. For Java it has a colon:

/function craft:my_house

Cheats must be on for the world (Creative worlds have them on already).

3. Where the build appears — and how to choose the spot yourself

It starts at your feet and grows east and south.

Face east — where the sun rises — and the build fills the ground in front of you and to your right. The app tells you how wide, deep and tall it is. Pick flat, open ground: the build is placed as-is, so over a hill part of it ends up inside the hill, and over a lake the water gets in.

In Java, F3 (on a Mac fn+F3) shows Facing. In Bedrock a compass or the map shows north; east is to its right.

Want a preview and exact control? Use the game's own structure block. It draws the build's outline before placing it, and you can nudge and rotate it.

1

Get a structure block

/give @s structure_block
2

Place it and open it

Switch the mode to Load. In the name field type your build's name: Java craft:my_house, Bedrock craft:my_house too.

3

Preview, adjust, load

Turn on the bounding box (Java: Show Bounding Box; Bedrock: it shows by default). Change the offsets and rotation until the outline sits where you want it, then press Load.

One structure block holds up to 48 blocks per side in Java and 64 in Bedrock. Bigger builds are exported as several parts; the app names them like my_house_0_0_48, and each goes at the offset in its name.

If something looks off

“Unknown function”

Java: the pack is not switched on yet — run the two lines from step 3. Bedrock: the pack is not activated on this world, or cheats are off. Also check the name: Java uses a colon (craft:my_house), Bedrock an underscore (craft_my_house), and spaces in your build's name become underscores.

A door, torch or flower is missing

They need solid ground under them. If the build was placed over a dip, a hole or water, the game drops them the moment they load. Place the build on flat ground, or fill the ground first.

Water is running everywhere

Your build has a pond or a pool and was placed above the ground, so the water has nowhere to sit. Place it lower, on the ground, and the water stays in its basin.

A corner stair is straight

The game decides a stair's corner shape from its neighbours, exactly as when you place one by hand. A corner stair with no stairs next to it is straight; in a finished roof it is a corner.

My animals are not there

Animals in Craft Block are decorations, not blocks. The game has no block for them, so they stay in the planner. The app tells you how many before you export.

The pack says “incompatible” (Java)

Enable it anyway — it loads. Older exports carry a version stamp the newest game does not recognise; exports from Craft Block 2.6.0 and later are marked for every version.
