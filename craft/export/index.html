Your build, in your own world

Six steps. One thing to do in each. At the end you type one command and the build appears in front of you. No mods.

What export does

Craft Block turns the build on your screen into a file the game understands. You open that file in Minecraft, type one command, and the same build appears in your world, block for block, at real size.

?

Where the button is

Home screen → press and hold a build → Export to Minecraft. Any build works: your own, or one of the examples.

?

Which edition do I play?

Bedrock if you installed Minecraft from the App Store, Google Play, the Microsoft Store, or play on Xbox, PlayStation or Switch. Java if you start it from the Minecraft Launcher on a PC or Mac. Not sure? On the game's title screen Bedrock shows a version like v1.21.x in the corner; Java shows Minecraft 1.21.x and the Launcher.

?

What you get

Bedrock: one file ending in .mcpack. Opening it puts a pack named after your build into the game. Java: one .zip ending in _datapack.zip that goes into your world's folder. Both are tiny and hold only your blocks, nothing else.

The app shows the size before you export: how many blocks, how wide, deep and tall. Bigger builds are cut into several parts inside the same file; the command still places all of them at once.

?

What carries over, and what does not

Carries over: every block, including stairs, slabs, doors, torches, glass, water, plants, with the direction you gave them.

Replaced: a few blocks exist only in Craft Block. The app lists each one and what the game gets instead (for example a lotus becomes a pink tulip). Nothing is dropped silently.

Stays in the app: animals and other creatures (they are decorations, the game has no block for them), and any texture pack you imported — the game uses its own textures.

?

Where it lands

Wherever you stand when you type the command. The build starts at your feet and grows east and south, so face east first. Details and a way to preview the exact spot are further down, in Where exactly does it appear?

?

How many times can I export?

Three times for free. After that, export is part of Craft Block Premium. The file itself never expires: once you have it, you can put the build into as many worlds as you like.

Bedrock: phone, tablet, console, Windows

Every step is one screen. The line under each says what you should see when it worked. The pictures are from an Android tablet; the screens look the same on iPhone, iPad and Windows.

1

Export from Craft Block

On the home screen press and hold your build → Export to Minecraft → Bedrock → Save .mcpack.

Then get the file into the game — this is the one step that differs by device:

Android phone or tablet: Minecraft opens by itself and imports the pack.

iPhone or iPad: a share sheet opens. Pick Minecraft. If it is not in the list, choose Save to Files, then open the Files app and tap the file.

Windows 10/11 (Bedrock from the Microsoft Store): send the file to the PC (mail, OneDrive, a cable) and double-click it. Minecraft starts and imports it.

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch: a console cannot open files. Import the pack on a phone or PC, put the build into a world there, and bring that world to the console through a Realm (Play on Realms → upload the world; the console then joins it). Or simply play that world on the phone.

You should see: Minecraft shows Import successful at the top.

Nothing happened, or the file opened somewhere else? Send the file to yourself (Telegram, Drive, mail), open it on the device, press and hold it → Open with → Minecraft.
2

Go to your worlds

In Minecraft press Play. You are on the Worlds tab.

New world: Create New → Create New World. Existing world: tap the pencil next to its name — not the name itself.

You should see: the world settings, with a list of sections on the left (General, Game, Resource Packs, Behavior Packs…).

3

Turn on cheats

Left list → Cheats → switch Cheats on (on older versions it sits under Game as Activate Cheats). The game warns that achievements will be off in this world — that is fine, say yes. While you are here, General → Game Mode → Creative makes building easier.

You should see: the Cheats switch green and “Achievements disabled” under the Play button.

4

Switch the pack on for this world

Left list → Behavior Packs → open My Packs → tap the pack with your build's name → Activate.

If the game asks Update world?, choose Add the pack anyway — it only warns that the world will contain something it did not make.

You should see: the pack moved up into Active.

No Activate button? You are inside a running world — that screen only shows packs. Leave the world (pause → Save & Quit) and come back through the pencil, as in step 2.
5

Enter the world and pick the spot

Press Create (new world) or Play (existing). Walk to flat, open ground. Turn so the sun is in front of you when it rises — that is east. The build will fill the ground in front of you and to your right.

You should see: a flat patch ahead, at least as big as the size the app showed.

6

Type the command

Tap the speech bubble at the top of the screen to open chat. Type the command from the app. It is two words with a space: the word function, then your build's name. For a build called My House:

/function craft_my_house

Press send. The build appears at once.

You should see: your build, exactly as in the app.

“Incorrect permission level for command: function” Cheats are on but your player is not an operator yet. Pause → Social (top right) → tap your own name → Operator. Type the command again.
“Unknown command: craft_my_house” The word function went missing — easy to lose on a touch keyboard. Two words: /function craft_my_house.
“Unknown function” Step 4 is not done for this world, or you are in a different world. Spaces in the build's name become underscores: My House → craft_my_house.

Java: PC and Mac

Java reads the build from a small .zip file you drop into your world's folder. Nothing to install.

1

Export from Craft Block

On the home screen press and hold your build → Export to Minecraft → Java → Save .zip. Send the file to your computer any way you like: AirDrop, mail, Telegram, a cable. Do not unzip it.

You should see: a file called my_house_datapack.zip on the computer.

2

Open your world's folder

Mac: in Finder press ⌘⇧G, paste ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves, Enter.
Windows: press WinR, type %appdata%\.minecraft\saves, Enter.
Linux: open ~/.minecraft/saves.

Open the folder with your world's name, then the datapacks folder inside it.

You should see: a folder named datapacks (it may be empty — that is normal).

3

Drop the .zip in

Move the file into datapacks. Still zipped.

You should see: the .zip sitting inside datapacks.

4

Switch the pack on

Open the world in the game. Press T for chat and type these two lines, one after the other. Use your file's name in the first one:

/datapack enable "file/my_house_datapack.zip" /reload

You should see: “Enabled data pack …” after the first line, and a short pause after the second.

Says the pack is “incompatible”? Enable it anyway, it loads. Exports from Craft Block 2.6.0 and later do not show this.
Commands do not work? Cheats are off for this world. Pause → Open to LAN → Allow Cheats: ON → Start LAN World. Creative worlds have cheats on already.
5

Pick the spot

Walk to flat, open ground. Press F3 (Mac: fnF3) and turn until the line Facing says east. The build will fill the ground in front of you and to your right.

6

Type the command

Press T and type the command from the app. In Java it has a colon. For a build called My House:

/function craft:my_house

You should see: your build, exactly as in the app.

“Unknown function” Step 4 was skipped, or the name is off: Java uses a colon and spaces become underscores — My House → craft:my_house.

Where exactly does it appear?

At your feet, growing east and south.

Face east (the sun rises there) and the build fills the ground in front of you and to your right. The app tells you how wide, deep and tall it is. The build is placed as-is: over a hill, part of it ends up inside the hill; over a lake, the water gets in. Flat ground first, command second.

I want a preview and exact control (structure block)

The game's own structure block draws the build's outline before placing it, and lets you nudge and rotate it. Get one with /give @s structure_block, place it, open it, set the mode to Load, type your build's name in the name field (craft:my_house in both editions), turn on the bounding box, adjust the offsets until the outline sits right, then Load. One block holds up to 48 blocks per side in Java and 64 in Bedrock; bigger builds come as several parts and the app lists the offset of each.

If something looks off

A door, torch or flower is missing

They need solid ground under them. If the build landed over a dip, a hole or water, the game drops them the moment they load. Place it on flat ground, or fill the ground first.

Water is running everywhere

Your build has a pond or a pool and was placed above the ground, so the water had nowhere to sit. Place it lower, on the ground, and the water stays in its basin.

A corner stair came out straight

The game decides a stair's corner shape from its neighbours, exactly as when you place one by hand. A corner stair with no stairs next to it is straight; in a finished roof it is a corner.

My animals are not there

Animals in Craft Block are decorations, not blocks. The game has no block for them, so they stay in the planner. The app tells you how many before you export.

I play on Xbox, PlayStation or Switch

Consoles cannot open files, so the pack cannot be imported there directly. Import it on a phone, tablet or PC (step 1 above), place the build in a world, then upload that world to a Realm and join the Realm from the console. Without a Realm, the build stays on the device you imported it on.

Is export free?

The first three exports are free. After that, export is part of Craft Block Premium. Everything on this page is done by the game itself and costs nothing.
