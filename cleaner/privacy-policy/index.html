Ultimate Cleaner — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 10, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Ultimate Cleaner collects, uses, and protects your information.

By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. As a parent, you should know that through the service, children under the age of 13 may participate in activities that involve the collection or use of personal information.

We use reasonable efforts to ensure that before we collect any personal information from a child, the child's parent receives notice of and consents to our personal information practices.

We may ask a user to verify their date of birth before collecting any personal information.

If the user is under the age of 13, the service may be blocked or redirected to a parental consent process.
