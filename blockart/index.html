Block Art for Minecraft

Turn any photo into pixel art built from real blocks. See exactly which blocks you need, build it in survival row by row, or open it in Minecraft as a ready world.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Everything happens on your device · no account needed

Your photo, block by block

Pets, faces, landscapes, logos. Pick a photo and watch it turn into a wall of wool, concrete, terracotta and more.

🎨

Real block colours

Every pixel is matched to a real block in a colour space tuned to the eye, so faces and skies stay smooth.

📏

From 32 to 256 blocks

Small and simple or huge and detailed. Photo style for detail, Clean style for easy building.

🧱

Exact block list

How many of each block, in stacks of 64, for the whole picture or for each 16 × 16 section.

🛠️

Survival build guide

Row by row, with runs like “5 × black wool”, world coordinates and saved progress.

🌍

Open it in Minecraft

A ready Bedrock world with your picture already built, an add-on pack, or a Java datapack.

🖨️

Printable pattern

A PDF with every section on its own page: numbered blocks, rows and columns.

How it works

1

Pick a photo

From your gallery or the camera, then frame it.

2

Choose the look

Size, style and blocks. Tap any block to see what it is.

3

Build it

Export to Minecraft, or follow the guide in survival.
