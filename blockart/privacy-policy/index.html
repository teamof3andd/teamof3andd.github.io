Block Art for Minecraft — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Block Art for Minecraft (“the App”) turns your photos into pixel art made of Minecraft blocks. It is built so that your photos and pictures stay on your device.

1. Your Photos

When you choose or take a photo, the App reads it only to make your picture. All processing happens on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The App can only see the photos you pick; on iOS you can limit access to selected photos at any time in Settings.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves your pictures (a copy of the photo you used, your frame and settings, and a small preview), your survival build progress and coordinates, and your preferences. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete a picture or delete the App. It may be included in your device's own backups, which are managed by Apple or Google under their policies.

3. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information, analytics or advertising identifiers. We receive information only if you choose to contact us by email, in which case we receive your email address and whatever you write.

4. Sharing and Exports

When you share a picture, a block list or a printable pattern, or export a world or pack to Minecraft, the file is passed to the app or person you choose through your device's share sheet. We do not see or store these files.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders on your device. They are local notifications: no server is involved and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the App or in your device settings.

6. Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. This policy will be updated before any new data processing is introduced.

7. Links

The App contains links to our website, the App Store and Google Play. Those services have their own privacy policies.

8. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete any email correspondence we hold about you by contacting us at the address below. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the rights provided by the GDPR and equivalent laws, including the right to complain to your supervisory authority.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this page, with its effective date shown above.

11. Contact
